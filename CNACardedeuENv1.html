The idea

The same circuit, from the air

Your own brand says it best: Tu viaje por El Salvador, conectado en un solo circuito. Today that circuit, from the lake to the coast and from the volcano to the city, is traveled by road. We operate the helicopter that can fly it.

It is no forced coincidence: several of the destinations your Explora program recommends to the guest, Lake Coatepeque, the La Libertad coastline and the volcano route, are the same ones our passengers already fly over. Your guests and our passengers are the same people; today they arrive by separate roads.

Commercial model

Your brand sells, we fly

We operate on a net-rate basis: we set a rate per flight and you define the final price to your guest, adding your margin on that base. The revenue sits on your side from the first sale, only flights actually flown are invoiced, and the terms are set out in writing in the agreement.

Execution is our responsibility: scheduling, passenger reception, weight and balance control and operation of the flight. You sell the experience under your brand and your standard; we answer for the air operation.

 

Transparency

Terms we make clear from the start

We operate one aircraft. We fly on advance reservation, not on demand. How a rescheduling for maintenance or weather is handled is one of the points we would like to define with you in the meeting, and it is set out in writing in the agreement.

Departures are from Helipuerto Salamanca. Operating to any other point requires a prior site survey and the owner's authorization. We do not confirm a landing before that review.

Every operation is subject to weight and balance and to weather conditions. The passenger receives the same explanation you receive.
