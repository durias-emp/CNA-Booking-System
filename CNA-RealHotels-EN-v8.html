Business model

Additional revenue with no investment

We work on a commission model over flights actually operated. The hotel buys no inventory, advances no capital and takes on no volume commitment. Commission is settled only on completed operations.

Execution is our responsibility: scheduling, guest reception, weight and balance control and flight operation. The hotel keeps a single coordination channel.

Ground transfer between the property and Helipuerto Salamanca can be arranged through the hotel, so the guest stays within your service chain from start to finish.

If any of your properties has a space suitable for landing, or if together we identify a closer helipad, that leg gets shorter and the direct airport transfer becomes a far more sellable product. It is something we would gladly assess with you.

 

Transparency

Conditions we make clear from the start

We operate one aircraft. Under scheduled maintenance or adverse weather, the flight is rescheduled. We do not commit to availability we cannot deliver.

Departures are from Helipuerto Salamanca. Operating from any other location requires a prior site survey and the owner’s authorization. We do not confirm a landing before that review.

Every operation is subject to weight and balance and to weather conditions. Outside operating limits, the flight is rescheduled. The guest receives the same explanation we give the hotel.

Cancellation and rescheduling policies. We review them in detail with you at the meeting, together with the terms of the alliance.
