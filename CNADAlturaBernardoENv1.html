How it fits

D’Eventos as a stop on the route

One operational note before the flights: the leg from Helipuerto Salamanca to your property is very short. That is why the flight to D’Eventos does not work as a product on its own: it works as part of something bigger.

There are two natural ways to sell it: inside a complete event package, where the helicopter arrival is the moment that opens or closes the experience, or as a stop on one of the routes, with D’Eventos as the tour’s landing point. In both cases the flight is quoted on the hourly rate, as detailed below.
